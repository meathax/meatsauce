### 2026-10-10 — Audio distortion: framework CDC freeze (root cause) and SPU deadline click

Report: audio "a bit distorted and not very clear". Root cause: `cs_audio_interp` ramped AUDIO_L/R linearly every clk_sys between 44.19 kHz samples, but `sys/audio_out.sv` crosses into clk_audio (24.576 MHz) by accepting a value only when two consecutive clk_audio samples are equal (`cl1<=core_l; cl2<=cl1; if(cl2==cl1) cl<=cl2`).
