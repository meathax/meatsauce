# Sheriff sound enable and mixed controls

The current wrapper updates both 74123 duration counters on one master edge. Starting mono0 clears mono1 in that update; when mono1 was active, `sn_enable=mono0||mono1` remains high. The SN module detects enabling edges with `previous_enabled`, so this transition does not restart its AD envelope.

MAME 0.289 performs two separate status updates: stop mono1, then start mono0. Its `enable_w` setter sees disabled then enabled at the same emulated time, resets AD and starts the internal one-shot. The recorded longer gameplay has 26 such extra restarts.
