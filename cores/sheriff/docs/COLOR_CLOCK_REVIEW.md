# Independent color-clock source review

The prior 3K CLK9=4J_QD claim is withdrawn. Fresh connected views trace CLK9 to HREAD_n, derived from **4I QD pin11 ->2L LS04 input5 ->output6**. Its positive edge is the falling edge of raw4I_QD, once per16 counter clocks. In the existing common-clock model, that is `ce_counter && phase_4i==15`; no upper_4j condition is supported.

The CLK wire crosses the 4J QB/QC/QD output risers without junction dots. It continues from sheet33 HREAD_n into sheet34, then turns downward near `(1868,2150)` and right into3K CLK9 near row2868. The split scan has about19–21 pixels of vertical displacement: the HREAD rail lies at sheet33 edge x5000,y2164–2169 and sheet34 x300,y2143–2149; surrounding rails match the same displacement. Fresh full joins and enlarged crossing/origin views are retained with hashes in `docs/evidence/sheriff_color_clock_review.json`. No neighboring 2K NAND or4K XOR output is substituted.

The parent's PROM aggregate finds nonuniform RGB in28/32 rows, up to6 RGB values per row. A staticmode0 latch held for a complete320-clock row cannot explain that variation. This is a falsifiable contradiction of the old model, not proof of a MAME bug. The independent wire trace supplies the replacement source identification.

Original clock/native reports are copied byte-for-byte under `_checks/agent-color-clock-review-20261010/withdrawn-4j-assumption/`. Their tests remain valid only for their assumed clock and do not qualify the withdrawn physical connection. Existing native artifacts/receipts remain at their original recovery paths. No production files are modified by this audit.

The corrected source clock does not by itself settle PROM/latch setup, hold or propagation: the same counter edge changes the address and clock nets.
